# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This directory contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, execute, communicate, and improve work across cross-functional efforts.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lightweight but structured project lifecycle designed to turn ideas into delivery outcomes with clear ownership and measurable progress.

### Core approach
- Customer-first delivery: prioritize customer value and usability.
- Iterative delivery: break work into shippable increments and learn quickly.
- Clear ownership: every initiative has a named project lead and product owner.
- Data-informed decision making: track metrics, risks, and evidence.
- Psychological safety: encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement.

### Lifecycle
1. Initiation: confirm the problem, stakeholders, goals, and high-level plan.
2. Planning: define scope, backlog, milestones, dependencies, and release approach.
3. Execution: build, test, review, and track progress with daily/weekly rhythms.
4. Release & deployment: verify readiness, deploy with controls, and communicate updates.
5. Retrospective & improvement: capture lessons and turn them into action items.

### Communication and governance
- Weekly syncs between project and product leads
- Delivery team standups to surface progress, blockers, and dependencies
- Risk tracking and escalation when issues cross team or business thresholds
- Clear acceptance criteria and Definition of Done before work is considered complete
